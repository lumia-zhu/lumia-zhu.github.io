Hi! I'm Zihao ZHU 👋

HCI Researcher  ·  City University of Hong Kong

I am currently a third-year Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science at the City University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Yuhan LUO.

My research interests lies at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Health Informatics, and Human-AI Collaboration, where I explore how AI can better support human well-being and daily productivity.

Before my Ph.D., I received an M.Phil. in Computational Media and Arts from HKUST(GZ) supervised by Prof. Pan HUI and Prof. Wei ZENG, and a Bachelor's degree from BUPT.
